# Parsnips
If you've never tried parsnips, you're in for a treat. The parsnip is a root vegetable that has an unusual and almost indescribable taste. It's an explosion of flavor in your mouth that's a cross between beets, butter, carrots, chickpeas, cookies, corn, potatoes, squash, sweet potatoes, and zucchini. Last, but not least, the texture is very much like a beet.
